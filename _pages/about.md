Hi👋, this is Junhyeok! I recently graduated with a double major in Computer Science and French Language & Literature. I research mechanistic interpretability of large language models, focusing on how internal representations can be identified, understood, and causally manipulated.

My work so far has centered on reproduction projects and independent experiments that probe the relationship between representational geometry and model behavior. I have reproduced [refusal direction ablation](/blog/2026/refusal-direction-reproduction/) to verify that a single direction in the residual stream mediates refusal, examined [linear truth structure](/blog/2026/geometry-of-truth-reproduction/) across multiple model families and scales using PCA, probing, and causal intervention, and applied the [Function Vectors methodology to zero-shot inference](/blog/2026/zero-shot_inference/) to derive relation vectors.

I am currently looking for Master's programs and research opportunities in mechanistic interpretability and AI safety. I am particularly interested in feature geometry, causal intervention, and how internal representations change with model scale.
